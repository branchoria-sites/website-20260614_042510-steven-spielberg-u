## Introduction

Few film locations have reshaped a real-world destination as thoroughly as [Devils Tower]({{ 'devils-tower/' | relative_url }}) after Steven Spielberg’s *[Close Encounters]({{ 'close-encounters/' | relative_url }}) of the Third Kind*. Before the film’s release in 1977, the Wyoming monolith was already a nationally significant geological landmark and the United States’ first national monument. Afterwards, it became something else as well: one of the most recognisable UFO destinations in popular culture. Visitors arrived not only to see an unusual rock formation but to stand where Spielberg imagined humanity’s contact with extraterrestrial visitors would occur. The result was a form of film tourism that permanently linked Devils Tower to UFO culture while leaving the monument’s geological, recreational and Indigenous meanings very much alive.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devils_Tower" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Devils Tower</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Devils Tower</span></span></span>

## From National Monument to Movie Emblem

The transformation of Devils Tower into a pop-culture destination happened unusually quickly. The climax of *Close Encounters* placed the monument at the centre of one of cinema’s most influential UFO narratives, turning its distinctive silhouette into an instantly recognisable image for audiences around the world. The site became inseparable from the film’s final encounter sequence, and visitors increasingly arrived because they already knew the landmark from the screen.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.intermountainhistories.org/items/show/226" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: intermountainhistories.org">[Intermountain Histories]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">intermountainhistories.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Intermountain HistoriesClose Encounters of the Third Kind and Devils Tower...Devils Tower is a National Monument situated in Wyoming an...</span></span></span>

The association proved durable rather than temporary. Decades after the film’s release, the National Park Service still highlights Devils Tower as one of the most prominent examples of a park location embedded in popular culture. Tourism organisations across Wyoming continue to market the monument through its connection to Spielberg’s film, recognising that many travellers first learned about the site through *Close Encounters*.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.nps.gov/subjects/parkpop/visit.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nps.gov">[National Park Service]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nps.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Park Service Visit</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National...8 Jul 2024 — Denali National Park (The River Wild, Into the Wild); Devils Tower National Monument (Close Encounters of the Th...</span></span></span><span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://travelwyoming.com/blog/stories/post/top-things-to-do-devils-tower-national-monument/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: travelwyoming.com">[Travel]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">travelwyoming.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Travel WyomingTop 12 Things to Do in Devils Tower National MonumentDec 5, 2025 — Devils Tower played a pivotal role in Spielberg&#x27;s 1977 c...</span></span></span>

The effect was large enough to become part of the monument’s own historical story. Accounts of Devils Tower’s cultural history routinely note that the film generated a substantial increase in visitation and climbing interest. What had been a famous geological feature became a destination associated with cinematic pilgrimage and UFO mythology.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devils_Tower" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Devils Tower</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Devils Tower</span></span></span>

## Why Visitors Remember the Tower

Film tourism usually fades when a movie leaves popular memory. Devils Tower has remained different because visitors encounter something that closely matches the image they already carry in their minds.

The monument’s unusual form is visible from miles away across the surrounding landscape. For many visitors, the experience involves a moment of recognition: they are not discovering an unfamiliar place but arriving at a landmark they feel they already know from the film. The location functions simultaneously as a natural wonder and as a cinematic icon.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://usatourist.com/articles/devils-tower-national-monument-travel-guide/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: usatourist.com">[USA Tourist]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">usatourist.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">USA Tourist Devils Tower National Monument Travel Guide</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">In the movie Devils Tower was...Read more...</span></span></span>

Visitor studies have shown that seeing the place associated with *Close Encounters of the Third Kind* is a specific motivation for many travellers. Alongside interests such as geology, wildlife and the monument’s historic status, the Spielberg connection remains one of the reasons people choose to make the journey.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://wpcdn.web.wsu.edu/wp-wpsites/uploads/sites/3019/docs/79_DETO_rept.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: wpcdn.web.wsu.edu">[WSU]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">wpcdn.web.wsu.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Devils Tower National Monument Visitor Studygeologic features (83%), visit the nation&#x27;s first national monument (40%), view wildlife (...</span></span></span>

Outdoor screenings of *Close Encounters* near the monument became a notable example of how tourism blended the real landscape with its fictional identity.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://travelwyoming.com/blog/stories/post/top-things-to-do-devils-tower-national-monument/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: travelwyoming.com">[Travel Wyoming]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">travelwyoming.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Travel WyomingTop 12 Things to Do in Devils Tower National MonumentDec 5, 2025 — Devils Tower played a pivotal role in Spielberg&#x27;s 1977 c...</span></span></span>

## Tourism, Climbing and Cultural Tension

The popularity generated by *Close Encounters* brought attention not only to sightseeing but also to recreation. Devils Tower became one of North America’s best-known climbing destinations, and the growth of climbing activity was frequently linked to the visibility created by the film.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devils_Tower" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Devils Tower</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Devils Tower</span></span></span>

Yet tourism at Devils Tower involves more than geology and cinema. The monument is also a sacred place for numerous Indigenous nations, including Lakota, Cheyenne and Kiowa communities. As visitor numbers increased and climbing became more popular, tensions emerged between recreational use and ceremonial use of the site.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devils_Tower" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Devils Tower</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Devils Tower</span></span></span>

The most visible response has been the voluntary June climbing closure established through the monument’s management plan. June is a period of particular cultural and spiritual importance for tribes associated with the site, and climbers are asked to refrain from ascending the Tower during that month.     Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
